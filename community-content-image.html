Facebook Image
অনুগ্রহ করে আগের Image-টিকে visual reference হিসেবে নিয়ে Facebook Feed-এ ব্যবহারের জন্য 1080 × 1350 px-এর একটি Image তৈরি করে দাও। RULE (MANDATORY) 1. Image dimensions: Exactly 1080 × 1350 px (4:5 aspect ratio). 2. Maintain a centrally positioned safe zone of 960 × 1230 px within the full image canvas. 3. Keep all essential content, including text, headlines, logos, product images and important visual elements, entirely within this safe zone. 4. Reserve the top 60 px, bottom 60 px, left 60 px and right 60 px as restricted areas. Do not place essential content in these areas. 5. Backgrounds, colours, gradients, textures and decorative elements may extend across the entire 1080 × 1350 px canvas. 6. Design the image specifically for Facebook Feed display, ensuring that the composition remains visually clear, readable, balanced and engaging. 7. Preserve the relevant visual identity, subject and design characteristics of the previous image while adapting the composition to these specifications.

Image dimensions: Exactly 1080 × 1920 px (9:16 aspect ratio). 2. Maintain a centrally positioned safe zone of 960 × 1420 px. 3. Keep all essential content, including text, headlines, logos, product images and important visual elements, entirely within this safe zone. 4. Reserve the top 250 px, bottom 250 px and left and right 60 px as restricted areas. Do not place essential content in these areas. 5. Backgrounds, colours, gradients, textures and decorative elements may extend across the entire canvas. 6. Design the image to remain visually clear, readable and compositionally balanced when shared as a WhatsApp Status, Channel Post or Community Group Post. 7. Preserve the relevant visual identity, subject and design characteristics of the previous image while adapting the composition to these specifications.
