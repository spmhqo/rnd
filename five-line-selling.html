Enter Your Call to Action

Enter a CTA to activate your prompts.
Step 2 · Setup prompt

Load Your Offer Into ChatGPT

Copy this setup prompt into a new ChatGPT chat. ChatGPT will then ask you to paste or upload your sales material, offer description, sales page, or URL.

Step 3 · Create three ads

Generate Your 3 Five-Line Ads

After ChatGPT loads your offer, copy this prompt into the same chat. Your CTA from Step 1 is already included automatically, so you don't need to enter it again.

ChatGPT generates all 3 ads. No need to copy all three back into this dashboard.
Step 4 · Paste one winning ad

Choose Your Favorite 5-Line Ad

Pick ONE complete ad from ChatGPT. Copy only the five ad lines, not the AD heading, and paste them into the single box below, then click Enter Selected Ad.

Paste your favorite ad and click Enter Selected Ad.
Step 5 · Create promotional content

Turn Your Ad Into More Promotions

After clicking Enter Selected Ad, click any creator to copy a fully written prompt containing your saved five-line ad. Paste the prompt into ChatGPT or another AI tool. Each prompt is self-contained, so you can use a fresh chat.
